COLOR DUEL 1.1 - GIAO DIEN MOI

Ban nay co hinh minh hoa dau truong sac mau, giao dien xanh dem,
the nguoi choi co avatar, o mau hieu ung khoi va dong ho vong tron.
Anh duoc dong goi trong JAR, khong can Internet de tai anh khi choi.

CACH CHUYEN TU BAN CU VA GIU TAI KHOAN

1. Dong cac cua so client va dung server cu.
2. Giai nen ban ZIP moi vao mot thu muc rieng.
3. Sao chep thu muc data cua ban cu sang thu muc ColorDuel cua ban moi.
   File data/state.bin chua tai khoan, diem va lich su cua nhom.
4. Mo demo-1may.bat de thu 1 server va 3 client tren cung may.
5. Dang nhap bang tai khoan da tao. IP 127.0.0.1, cong 5000.

Chay nhieu may: mo run-server.bat tren may server, run-client.bat
tren cac may con lai va nhap IPv4 cua server nhu ban truoc.
